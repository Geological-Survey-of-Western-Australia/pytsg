Citation
========

If you use :mod:`pytsg` in your research please cite it as:

    Chi, B. (2022). pytsg [Computer software]. https://github.com/FractalGeoAnalytics/pytsg

If you use the simple quadratic method (:func:`pytsg.feature.sqm`) for extracting the feature depth and position please cite:

    Rodger, A., Laukamp, C., Haest, M., & Cudahy, T. (2012). A simple quadratic method of
    absorption feature wavelength estimation in continuum removed spectra. Remote Sensing of
    Environment, 118, 273–283. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2011.11.025

If you use the outputs of the jCLST algorithm please cite:

    Green, A. A., Green, D., Mason, P., & Berman, M. (2025). Joint unmixing of SWIR and
    TIR reflectance spectra for mineral composition. Australian Journal of Earth Sciences,
    73(5), 720–729. https://doi.org/10.1080/08120099.2025.2464839
