Related Tools and Literature
============================

Academic Papers
---------------

The :term:`National Virtual Core Library` has had two special issues published in the Australian Journal of Earth Sciences. The
papers in these special issues not only describe the technical aspects of the infrastructure, instrumentation, and algorithms but
also the application of hyperspectral analysis with supporting case studies.

    Laukamp, C., Mulè, S., Lau, I. C., Pejcic, B., Stromberg, J., Warren, P., … Shelton, T. (2026). The AuScope National 
    Virtual Core Library infrastructure program—from vision to implementation. Australian Journal of Earth Sciences, 73(5),
    630–641. https://doi.org/10.1080/08120099.2026.2693906

    Huntington, J. (2016). Uncovering the mineralogy of the Australian Continent: The AuScope National Virtual Core Library.
    A national hyperspectrally derived drill-core archive. Australian Journal of Earth Sciences, 63(8), 923–928.
    https://doi.org/10.1080/08120099.2016.1269630

If you are interested in the use of HyLogger instrumentation in earth sciences please see:

    Schodlok, M. C., Whitbourn, L., Huntington, J., Mason, P., Green, A., Berman, M., … Martinez, R. (2016). HyLogger-3, a
    visible to shortwave and thermal infrared reflectance spectrometer system for drill core logging: functional description.
    Australian Journal of Earth Sciences, 63(8), 929–940. https://doi.org/10.1080/08120099.2016.1231133

There are many papers discussing the algorithms and spectral analytical techniques used in geoscience research and the minerals
industry:

    Green, A. A., Green, D., Mason, P., & Berman, M. (2026). Joint unmixing of SWIR and TIR reflectance spectra for mineral
    composition. Australian Journal of Earth Sciences, 73(5), 720–729. https://doi.org/10.1080/08120099.2025.2464839

    Stromberg, J. M., Laukamp, C., Hill, E. J., & Rodger, A. (2026). MyLogger: fast logging with HyLogger spectral mineralogy.
    Australian Journal of Earth Sciences, 73(5), 730–745. https://doi.org/10.1080/08120099.2026.2653999

    Laukamp C, Rodger A, LeGras M, Lampinen H, Lau IC, Pejcic B, Stromberg J, Francis N, Ramanaidou E. Mineral Physicochemistry
    Underlying Feature-Based Extraction of Mineral Abundance and Composition from Shortwave, Mid and Thermal Infrared Reflectance
    Spectra. Minerals. 2021; 11(4):347. https://doi.org/10.3390/min11040347

    Rodger, A., Fabris, A., & Laukamp, C. (2021). Feature Extraction and Clustering of Hyperspectral Drill Core Measurements to
    Assess Potential Lithological and Alteration Boundaries. Minerals, 11(2), 136. https://doi.org/10.3390/min11020136

    Rodger, A., & Ramanaidou, E. (2022). Deconstructing the Iron Boomerang—Quantitative Predictions of Hematite, Ochreous, and
    Vitreous Goethite Mixtures. Minerals, 12(3), 381. https://doi.org/10.3390/min12030381

Software
--------

The Spectral Geologist - The Spectral Geologist is the industry standard tool for the mineralogical analysis of VIS/NIR/SWIR/MIR
and TIR reflectance spectra. It has a paid option for analysing hyperspectral data, and free viewer for read-only access of 
TSG datasets.

    * https://research.csiro.au/thespectralgeologist/ 

`nvcl-kit` is a python package available via PyPI which provides programmatic access to :term:`NVCL` data directly from the geological
surveys.

    * https://auscope.github.io/nvcl_kit
    * https://pypi.org/project/nvcl-kit/

Data
----

There are several options available online for reference reflectance spectra including:

    * CSIRO Spectral Reflectance Database. https://luminescence.csiro.au/reflectance
    * Kokaly, R.F., Clark, R.N., Swayze, G.A., Livo, K.E., Hoefen, T.M., Pearson, N.C., Wise, R.A., Benzel, W.M., Lowers, H.A., Driscoll, R.L.,
      and Klein, A.J., 2017, USGS Spectral Library Version 7 Data: U.S. Geological Survey data release, https://doi.org/10.5066/F7RR1WDJ.

:term:`NVCL` HyLogger datasets can be downloaded from the state and territory geological surveys, and is available to search as a national
collection via the AuScope Discovery Portal.

    * https://portal.auscope.org.au/
